# Campaign Strategy, Aléxis "Lex" Almeida Tattoo Artist

**Client:** Alexis Almeida | Porto, Portugal
**Package:** Growth (SMM + SEM) + Starter (website)
**Start:** May 2026
**Monthly retainer:** EUR 750 + IVA (Growth EUR 450 | Starter EUR 300)
**Comms language:** English
**Primary goal:** Premium custom tattoo bookings in Porto that match Alexis's German income level

---

## Situation Summary

Alexis is a veteran tattoo artist with 11 years of experience who splits his time between Portugal and Germany. He is transitioning to base himself in Porto full-time and needs Portuguese clients who will pay what German clients pay for premium custom work. He has worked with multiple digital marketing agencies before and seen no results, making him appropriately skeptical. He will stay a client if and only if he can see a clear, unambiguous line between our work and money in his pocket.

He has a website (lextattoo.com), a Google My Business listing with 12 reviews at 5.0 stars, and an Instagram account (@alexis.tattoo.art) with an established portfolio. The brand is dark, gothic, skull-oriented. The work is fine line, blackwork, dark art, and neo-traditional collector-grade custom pieces.

### What this strategy is not

This is not a volume play. Alexis is not chasing walk-ins or cheap bookings. A single premium client who comes in for a sleeve or a back piece can generate EUR 2,000 to EUR 5,000+. The strategy is to find those people, collectors, enthusiasts with taste, people who have been thinking about a serious piece for months, and give them a compelling reason to choose Lex over every other tattoo artist in Porto.

Every piece of content, every ad, every copy line should reinforce one positioning: this is the best custom tattoo artist in Porto for people who care about the work.

## Conversion Tracking Setup

Alexis has lextattoo.com (Starter deliverable) which enables proper full-funnel conversion tracking. This is a major advantage over a websiteless client and must be set up before any paid campaigns go live.

**Conversion events to track (in Google Ads + GA4):**

1. Booking form submission, primary conversion (highest intent)
2. Phone click (tel: link), secondary conversion
3. WhatsApp click-to-chat link click, secondary conversion
4. Email link click (mailto:), tertiary conversion
5. Session duration > 2 minutes, micro-conversion (engagement signal)

**Setup steps:**

1. Install GA4 on lextattoo.com. Link GA4 to Google Ads account.
2. Create GA4 events for each form submission (form_submit event on the contact/booking form).
3. Mark form_submit as a key event in GA4.
4. Import GA4 key events into Google Ads as conversion actions.
5. Add Google Ads conversion linker tag via Google Tag Manager.
6. Set up phone call conversions via Google Ads call extensions (calls > 30 seconds count as a conversion).
7. Test every conversion before campaigns go live, verify in the Google Ads conversion test tool.

**Attribution model:** Data-driven (Google Ads default). Do not use last-click. Alexis's clients may research for weeks before booking, multi-touch attribution matters.

**Reporting cadence:** Every monthly report leads with conversions. Not impressions, not clicks, not CTR. Conversions and cost per conversion. This is the number that matters to a skeptical client.

## Managing Client Skepticism

Alexis has been burned by agencies before. The way to keep this client long-term:

- Never overpromise on timeline. At EUR 3/day, Month 1 is about data collection, not bookings. Set that expectation at the kickoff call.
- Show the work. Share the Google Ads dashboard read-only access so Alexis can see spend and conversions in real time. Transparency is the antidote to skepticism.
- Attribute clearly. When a booking comes in, ask "how did you find us?" The answer should start connecting to the UTM source data in GA4.
- Report on what changed. Every monthly report should explain what was adjusted and why. Show that the work is active and intelligent, not just "we ran some ads."
- Get to a conversion as fast as possible. One booked client from the ads in Month 1 or Month 2 will buy more trust than any presentation.
